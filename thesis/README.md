# Thesis-style monograph

**Title:** Weather Stress, Yield Risk and Price Discovery in Arabica Coffee Futures  
**Author:** Liam Son  
**Date:** 10 September 2026  
**Status:** Complete working-paper monograph written to doctoral structure. Not an awarded degree.

The formatted manuscript (DOCX and PDF) is generated from the research design, methodology, and registered 2015–2025 engine test in this repository.

## What is finished

- Research questions RQ1–RQ5 and hypotheses
- Identification strategy and threats table
- Measurement system (ERA5 multi-point EHD / HDD / precip anomaly)
- Overlay protocol with lag and EWMA vol targeting
- Registered Table 6.1 (engine test)
- Explicit list of unestimated objects (CONAB yields, roll series, DSR on headline Sharpe)
- References and reproduction appendix
