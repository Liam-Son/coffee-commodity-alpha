# Selected references

Working bibliography for the design notes. Not an exhaustive literature review.

## Agricultural weather and coffee

- Bunn, C., Läderach, P., Rivera, O. O., & Kirschke, D. (2015). A bitter cup: climate change profile of global production of Arabica and Robusta coffee. *Climatic Change*.
- DaMatta, F. M., et al. Work on Coffea arabica thermal limits and drought physiology (various).
- CONAB. Brazilian coffee crop surveys (successive *Safra Café* releases).
- USDA FAS. *Coffee: World Markets and Trade* and GAIN Brazil annuals.

## Weather risk premia and futures

- Li, Z., Hayes, D. J., & Jacobs, K. L. (2017). The weather premium in the U.S. corn market. *Journal of Futures Markets*.
- Vizcarrondo, E., & Janzen, J. (2023). The weather risk premium in coffee futures prices. *farmdoc daily*.

## Inference for short dependent samples

- Newey, W. K., & West, K. D. (1987). A simple, positive semi-definite, heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation consistent covariance matrix. *Econometrica*.
- Künsch, H. R. (1989). The jackknife and the bootstrap for general stationary observations. *Annals of Statistics*.
- Politis, D. N., & Romano, J. P. (1994). The stationary bootstrap. *Journal of the American Statistical Association*.

## Backtest evaluation and multiple testing

- Lo, A. W. (2002). The statistics of Sharpe ratios. *Financial Analysts Journal*.
- Bailey, D. H., & López de Prado, M. (2014). The deflated Sharpe ratio. *Journal of Portfolio Management*.
- White, H. (2000). A reality check for data snooping. *Econometrica*.
- Harvey, C. R., Liu, Y., & Zhu, H. (2016). … and the cross-section of expected returns. *Review of Financial Studies*.

## Weather derivatives and index insurance

- Jewson, S., & Brix, A. (2005). *Weather Derivative Valuation*. Cambridge.
- Stoppa, A., & Hess, U. (2003). Design and rating of weather-based index insurance schemes.
- Empirical coffee parametric programmes: Blue Marble *Café Seguro*; ECOM / WTW Vietnam layered rainfall covers (see research note on parametric insurance).
