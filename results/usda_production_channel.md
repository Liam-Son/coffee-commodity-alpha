# USDA Brazil Arabica production channel (annual)

Source: USDA PSD Arabica production, thousand 60 kg bags, compiled via IndexMundi.

## Series (levels)

2010 41.8m … 2020 49.7m … **2021 36.4m (−26.8%)** … 2024 45.4m.

The 2021 crop year is the dominant left-tail event in the modern sample and coincides with the widely documented frost/drought episode in Minas Gerais.

## Why this is not yet a yield regression

N = 15 annual observations. Biennial bearing creates a mechanical oscillation that will confound any weather regressor that is not residualised against a two-year cycle. Production is not yield: area and tree vintage move. USDA marketing years are not calendar years. Therefore this file is a **control series and a narrative check**, not an estimate of β1 in the yield equation.

## Narrative check against the index

The overlay is designed to be long coffee when lagged Brazilian weather stress is high. 2021 is the year that must appear as a high-stress episode if the measurement system is not nonsense. The engine-test stress range includes values up to 2.79 after the one-month lag; the production data confirm that the same crop year was a −0.31 log shock to national Arabica output. That is consistency, not identification.
