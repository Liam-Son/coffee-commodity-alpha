## Interpretation

1. The weather-stress engine runs end-to-end on real multi-point ERA5 data with a proper 1-month lag.
2. Without vol targeting the strategy delivers a higher Sharpe than buy-and-hold (0.45 vs 0.38) and roughly half the max drawdown.
3. A 10% vol target is conservative relative to KC realised volatility → average weight ≈ 0.33, which compresses both return and risk.
4. Raising the vol target to 15–20% is the most direct way to recover absolute return while retaining drawdown control.
